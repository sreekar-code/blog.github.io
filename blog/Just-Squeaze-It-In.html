Just squeaze it in

28 Jul, 2026

I had 45 minutes before a call. Not enough time for a proper gym session, right?

Wrong.

I went anyway. Did what I could. Came back just in time. It wasn't perfect, but it was enough.

That's been my approach lately — squeeze it in. Don't wait for the perfect two-hour window. Don't wait for the ideal mood. Don't wait until the calendar clears up.

Got 20 minutes? Write a few tweets.

Got 15 minutes? Read a few pages.

Got 10 minutes? Sit and think. Actually think — no phone, no tab-switching.

The big blocks rarely come. And when they do, they're often swallowed by things you didn't plan for. Meetings that run long. Conversations that needed to happen. Life.

But the small gaps? They're always there. And most of us let 'em go. Scroll a bit. Zone out. Wait for the "right time."

I used to do that too.

Now I try to squeeze. Not frantically — I'm not turning every spare moment into a productivity sprint. But when I notice a window and there's something I've been meaning to do, I ask: can I do a version of this right now?

Usually, the answer is yes.

That gym session wasn't ideal. But I moved. My body reminded itself that it knows how to move. And that mattered.

I just did it. And will continue to do it.
