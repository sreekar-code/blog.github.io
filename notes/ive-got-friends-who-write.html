I've got friends who write

30 Jul, 2026

After a looong day, when you are dead tired and lying on the bed, waiting for sleep to consume you, you receive a message.

You open it. And you find an image of heavens-above beautiful writeup, all handwritten on paper.

Those words just fill your heart.

That's exactly what happened with me today.

One of my friends wrote a piece and shared it with me. I read it and I was at a loss for words. It's achingly beautiful!

Now, I feel fortunate, grateful, and happy.

Fortunate, 'coz I've got friends who write, in the midst of machines spewing gibberish all day.

Grateful, 'coz I've got friends who share their poems and writeups with me.

Happy, 'coz I've got friends who find comfort in sharing their writeups with me.

What else do I need? I've got friends who write!
