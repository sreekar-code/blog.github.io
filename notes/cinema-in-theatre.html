Cinema in theatre

06 Aug, 2026

I'm falling in love with cinema in theatre.

Past five weeks, I watched at least one movie in theatre every Saturday/Sunday. And I'm planning to watch one more this weekend.

I'm even considering buying a Cinepolis card. I'll mostly get it this week.

But why am I so in love with cinema in theatre lately?

Because it has become my Narnia door now. For three hours, I can just be in a different world. A world where I can let go of every thought that's weighing me down.

This isn't something new I've discovered. Many people have already found this Narnia door in theatre. I just happened to find it now.

Actually, I did find it before. In books. But now, in theatre too.

To cinema in theatre 🥂
